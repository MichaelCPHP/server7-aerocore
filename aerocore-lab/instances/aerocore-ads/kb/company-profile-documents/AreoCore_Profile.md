# AreoCore Inc.

## Company Overview

AreoCore Inc. specializes in the recovery and redistribution of high-performance materials used in precision machining and aerospace applications. We work directly with CNC machine shops, manufacturers, and fabrication facilities to acquire used or end-of-life materials that still hold significant recoverable value.

## What We Do

We focus on sourcing worn, used, or surplus carbide tooling and other high-performance materials, including end mills, inserts, drills, and reamers. Our goal is to provide a streamlined solution for businesses to efficiently manage and monetize their excess or unusable materials.

## Materials We Work With

- Tungsten Carbide (End Mills, Inserts, Drills)
- Inconel
- Titanium
- High-Temperature Alloys (Hastelloy, Monel)
- Tool Steels (H13, D2, etc.)

## Who We Work With

We partner with precision machining companies, aerospace suppliers, medical manufacturers, and metal fabrication shops that generate high volumes of tooling wear and material scrap.

## Our Approach

AreoCore positions itself as a professional material recovery partner rather than a traditional scrap buyer. We prioritize long-term relationships, consistent pickups, and transparent pricing based on current market conditions.

## Value Proposition

- Turn unused materials into immediate revenue
- Simplify disposal and recovery processes
- Provide consistent and reliable pickups
- Maintain a professional, service-oriented partnership

## Mission

To become a trusted partner in high-performance material recovery by providing efficient, transparent, and scalable solutions for manufacturers and machining facilities.
