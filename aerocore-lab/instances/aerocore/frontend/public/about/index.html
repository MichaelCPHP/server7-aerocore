About AeroCore Material Recovery

Built for Aerospace & Precision Manufacturing

Built for Aerospace & Precision Manufacturing

AeroCore is not a generic recycler. We are specialists in high-value material recovery, focused exclusively on the materials that come out of aerospace production, CNC machining, medical device manufacturing, and precision tooling operations. We understand the difference between a C2 straight-grade carbide insert and a C6 steel-cutting grade — and we price them accordingly.

Our direct relationships with domestic and international processors eliminate middlemen from the supply chain, which means more money in your pocket for every pound of material you sell. Whether you have a coffee can of used end mills or a full warehouse of production surplus, we provide fast, transparent quotes backed by real market data.

Why AeroCore

What Sets Us Apart

Six reasons manufacturers and machine shops choose AeroCore for material recovery.

Aerospace Expertise

We understand superalloy grades, specifications, and the value differentials between materials that look similar but have vastly different compositions. From Inconel 718 to Waspaloy, Ti-6Al-4V to L-605, we know what your material is worth.

Top Market Prices

Our direct-to-processor relationships cut out the middlemen. You get pricing based on real commodity indices — not a discounted rate from a broker who takes a cut before your material reaches the refinery.

1-Hour Quotes

Time is money. When you reach out with photos and an estimated weight, we respond with a competitive quote within 1 business hour. No waiting days for a callback or dealing with voicemail systems.

Any Quantity

No minimums, no maximums. Whether you have 5 pounds of used carbide inserts from your tool crib or 5,000 pounds from a plant closure, we buy it all. Every inquiry gets the same professional attention.

Nationwide Logistics

Located anywhere in the United States? No problem. We provide prepaid shipping labels for smaller lots and arrange freight pickup for larger shipments. You do not need to be local to get top prices from AeroCore.

Transparent Process

No hidden deductions, no surprise fees, no vague explanations. We tell you exactly how we evaluate your material, what market prices we reference, and how we arrive at your quote. If something changes, we contact you first.

Industries

Industries We Serve

AeroCore works with manufacturers, shops, and contractors across a range of high-performance industries.

Aerospace

Superalloy turnings, rejected components, end-of-life tooling, and production surplus from aircraft and engine manufacturers.

CNC & Machine Shops

Used carbide tooling, broken end mills, worn inserts, and surplus material from daily machining operations. Your end-of-life tooling has real value.

Medical Device

Titanium, cobalt-chrome, and specialty alloy surplus from implant manufacturing, surgical instrument production, and prototype machining.

Oil & Gas

Carbide wear parts, tungsten carbide drill bits, Inconel components, and high-temperature alloys from downhole and surface equipment manufacturing.

Defense

Tungsten alloys, depleted uranium alternatives, armor-grade materials, and precision-machined components from defense manufacturing programs.

Tool & Die

Carbide die components, worn punches, D2 and A2 tool steels, and tungsten carbide wear plates from stamping and forming operations.
