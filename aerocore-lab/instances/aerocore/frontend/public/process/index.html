Checklist

What to Include With Your Quote Request

The more information you provide upfront, the faster and more accurate your quote will be.

Photos Showing Material Type

Clear photographs are the single most helpful thing you can include. Show the material from multiple angles so we can identify the type and grade. Close-ups of markings or brand stamps are especially useful.

Estimated Total Weight

An approximate weight helps us prepare an accurate quote. If you're unsure, a rough estimate (e.g., "about 50 lbs" or "two 5-gallon buckets") works fine.

Material Description

Let us know what you have: carbide end mills, inserts, tungsten rods, Inconel turnings, titanium bar stock, etc. Include condition details — new, used, broken, or mixed.

Company Name (Optional)

If you're reaching out on behalf of a business, including the company name helps us set up recurring pickups and volume-based pricing arrangements.

Preferred Contact Method

Let us know if you prefer to be contacted by phone, email, or text so we can reach you in the way that's most convenient.

Tips for Packing Carbide Safely

  • Use a sturdy double-walled cardboard box or 5-gallon bucket with a secure lid.
  • Wrap sharp cutting tools in newspaper or bubble wrap to prevent punctures.
  • Keep different material types separated if possible (carbide, HSS, steel).
  • Ensure the box is rated for the weight — carbide is very heavy for its size.
  • Seal all seams with packing tape and clearly label the package.
